Parat

Parat shows the barcodes of your loyalty cards at the till, fast and without a connection.

  • Offline at the till. The barcodes are drawn on the iPhone.
  • No account, no tracking. The app collects no data.
  • Your cards. On the iPhone and in your own iCloud.

Parat is in testing and not yet in the App Store. In the test versions, iCloud sync is still off.
