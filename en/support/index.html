Frequently asked questions

The code does not scan at the till. What can I do?

In the settings, turn on “Boost brightness”: the screen gets bright and stays on while a card is open. For a long code, “Show rotated” helps. If you took the code from a retailer’s app, it can change there. The safest number to store is the one on the plastic card.

How do I add a card without a camera?

Tap “Add card”. Then choose “Import from images” to pick screenshots, or “Enter number” to enter number and format by hand.

Which code formats work?

EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-E, Code 128, Code 39, ITF, Codabar, QR, Aztec and PDF417. The app stores the number and the format exactly as they were read.

What happens to my cards on a new iPhone?

The cards are on the iPhone where you saved them. Syncing through your own iCloud is still off in the test versions. A new iPhone therefore starts without cards.

How do I delete a card?

In the card list, swipe the row to the left and tap “Delete”. The app asks first.

Does the app send my card numbers anywhere?

No. The app draws barcodes on the device. A card number goes only to your own iCloud and to no other service.
